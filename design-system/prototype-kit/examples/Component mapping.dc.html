Component mapping

Every component used in the Selection, Manage Audition and Sedcard (Summary view) prototypes, from the Bootstrap 3.4 name in the current product to its Bootstrap 6 replacement, with the v6 component rendered live. The v6 column and the examples come from the Filmmakers System Library, which is the source of truth.

SF-3
dropdown of checkboxes
.dropdown + .checkbox
Combobox
.combobox-toggle + .menu + .check / .radio
Replace. Multi-select filter: the toggle shows the label and the selection (“Language: 2 selected”), the menu holds one checkbox per value, and a .combobox-search input appears once the list passes six entries. One control for every filter: single-value filters carry radio items, multi-value filters checkbox items, and the toggle always reads label plus value. Replaces the native select the product used before (FRM-2, retired), including the Sort by control. Two deviations to settle: the checkbox is nested inside label.menu-item rather than paired by for/id (see FRM-9), and it resets --bs-theme-bg / --bs-theme-contrast because a themed .menu parent would otherwise paint the unchecked box in the menu's own fill.
